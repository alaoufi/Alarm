## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)
Alerts is an Arabic-first reminders and alarm app built to fire reliably — even on aggressive battery-management phones (Huawei, Xiaomi, Oppo…) — so you never miss what matters.

★ Alarms you can't miss
• Loud, full-screen alerts that appear over the lock screen.
• "Can't-miss" mode: the sound stops only after you solve a quick challenge (a number or a word) — no accidental snooze or dismiss.
• Auto-raise the alarm volume even in silent mode, gradually if you prefer.

★ Quiet hours
• Set daily windows (e.g. sleep) where no sound plays — the notification still shows clearly, without disturbing you.

★ Flexible scheduling
• Daily / weekly / monthly / yearly repeats, plus a Hijri calendar.
• Pre-alerts, plus location and attachment reminders.

★ Medication management 💊
• Schedule doses every N hours or days, with a course by number of doses or days.
• Dose log and adherence tracking.

★ Privacy & security
• Fully offline — your data stays on your device.
• Biometric lock (fingerprint/face) and encrypted backups.

★ Backup & sync
• Automatic local backups, with optional Google Drive sync.

★ Extra polish
• 14 languages, elegant themes and colors, a detailed user guide, and a "Today" board.

Trial & subscription:
• 10-day free trial with all features.
• Then a monthly, semi-annual, or yearly subscription via Google Play.
• When a subscription ends, all your data is kept safe and returns as soon as you renew.
